VSTM Tune

Real-time vocal pitch correction. This installer puts the following in the right folders:

  • VST3 plug-in → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
    FL Studio, REAPER, Ableton Live, Cubase, Studio One …
  • Audio Unit (AU) → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
    Logic Pro, GarageBand
  • Standalone app (works without a DAW) → /Applications

To install only some of them, click Customize in the next step. The installer asks for an administrator password.

Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or later. The plug-in is not signed with an Apple Developer ID.
